The problem

Experiments show what AI can do. They don't decide where to invest.

Pilots and tool purchases multiply while the business stays the same. Leadership teams usually face three connected gaps, and none of them are solved by technology alone.

01 · Investment

No focused set of choices

Use cases and local productivity gains haven't become clear decisions about revenue, cost, capacity or customer outcomes.

02 · Business design

The organisation hasn't moved

Products, workflows, roles and decision-making haven't adapted to the capabilities being introduced.

03 · Execution

No one owns the result

Initiatives lack the ownership, capability or coordination needed to deliver measurable results.

How we work

Five stages, each worth having on its own

The work moves from a decision to a transformation your team can run without us. Every stage produces outputs you own, and you can stop after any of them.

  1. 01

    Decide

    Choose the few opportunities worth pursuing and set out an initial value hypothesis for each.

  2. 02

    Design

    Redesign products, workflows, roles and decision rights around those opportunities.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Test the riskiest assumptions against agreed measures, then proceed, adapt or stop.

  4. 04

    Transform

    Mobilise and deliver the changes that create the value.

  5. 05

    Transfer

    Leave your team with the capability and operating rhythm to carry on without us.
