Why it stalls

The technology usually isn't what's holding you back.

In BCG's 2026 CEO survey, most companies were running AI pilots, but only about a quarter had changed the wider business around them. Buying more tools fixes none of the usual gaps.

  • No link to the P&L. Use cases and local time savings haven't turned into clear decisions about revenue, cost or customers.
  • The organisation hasn't moved. Products, workflows, roles and decisions still work the way they did before.
  • No one owns the result. Initiatives lack the owner, the skills or the coordination to deliver something measurable.

How we work

From decision to results you can see:

The work moves from choosing where AI pays off to a team that runs the change and can show the results. Every stage produces outputs you own, and you can stop after any of them.

  1. 01

    Decide

    We sit down with your leadership team and pick the few AI opportunities worth funding. Each one gets a business case in numbers and the measures we'll judge it by.

  2. 02

    Design

    Together we rework the products, workflows, roles and decisions those opportunities touch, and agree which calls stay with people.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    We test the riskiest assumptions in small, time-boxed pilots first. What works, your teams and partners build and scale, with us leading alongside them.

  4. 04

    Embed

    Each result gets a named owner on your side. We train the people doing the new work and track it against the agreed measures until your team runs it without us.
