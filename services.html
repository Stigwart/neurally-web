Fit

Who we work with

CEOs, founders, and product and technology leaders of scaling and established businesses of roughly 50 to 1,000 people. Typically AI could change your costs, speed or products, but it isn't clear how to get from experiments to real change.

The strongest fit

  • Real product, service or operational complexity
  • Enough scope for operating-model change to affect performance
  • Direct access to a CEO and leadership team able to act on decisions
  • Most often financial services & insurance, marketplaces & e-commerce, or B2B SaaS, and increasingly professional services

What a useful engagement needs

  • An executive sponsor
  • Access to the relevant evidence and people
  • Budget for the agreed work
  • Willingness to change how the business operates

We confirm all of this during scoping, before a fee is agreed.

Who does what

We lead the agreed diagnosis, design, executive alignment and transformation leadership, and support implementation and training hands-on. Your teams bring business knowledge and deliver the changes assigned to them. Specialist partners do the build where needed, with responsibilities agreed before delivery begins.

Common questions

Before you get in touch

  1. 01Are you an AI consultancy? Not quite. AI is usually why people call us, but most of the work is on the business itself: its economics, products, organisation and delivery.
  2. 02Do you implement things? Yes. We lead agreed transformation work and support implementation hands-on: specifying pilots, choosing and briefing build partners, and working alongside your teams on the first releases. Your teams and specialist partners write the software. Scope, decision rights, resources and accountability are agreed before work begins.
  3. 03How are you different from a large consultancy? You work directly with a senior operator who can move from board-level choices into product, operating-model and delivery detail. Small, accountable and practical, with specialists added only where the problem needs them.
  4. 04Can we start with training or a build? Yes, if the problem and the expected value are already clear. We offer both, tied to your own priorities rather than off the shelf. If leadership still needs to decide where to focus, the free call, Workshop or Diagnostic come first.
  5. 05Do I have to book the free call before anything else? No, but most people do. It's a fast way to check fit and agree whether a Workshop, Diagnostic or Deep Dive is the right next step, with no fee attached.
  6. 06What's the difference between the Workshop, the Diagnostic and the Deep Dive? The Workshop (one day, £2,500) gives your leadership team a shared view of priorities and a recommendation on what to do next. The Diagnostic (one week, £6,000) goes deeper on one or two priority areas. The Deep Dive (two weeks, £12,500) covers the whole business, adds an operating-model outline and a board-ready mobilisation plan.
  7. 07Does the Workshop fee count towards later work? Yes. If you book a Diagnostic or Deep Dive within 60 days of the Workshop, its £2,500 fee is credited in full against it.
