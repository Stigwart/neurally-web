Services

Four ways to work together

Most clients start with the AI Advantage Diagnostic. From there you can commission design, transformation leadership or continuing advice. Each engagement is scoped on its own and produces outputs you keep.

01 · The starting point

AI Advantage Diagnostic

For a CEO and leadership team that need to decide where AI merits investment and what must change to realise the value.

Typically three weeks From £12,500. Fixed fee confirmed after a scoping conversation.

The work

  • Leadership alignment and strategic context
  • Review of customer value, products, priority workflows and current AI activity
  • Opportunity, economics, readiness and dependency assessment
  • What should stay human, be augmented, be automated or be removed
  • Prioritisation and an executive decision session

You receive

  • A prioritised set of AI opportunities within the agreed scope
  • An initial value hypothesis, key assumptions and proposed success measures for each priority
  • An initial map of how priority work could change, including human, AI and automation responsibilities
  • A readiness, risk and dependency assessment
  • A 90-day mobilisation plan with owners and decision gates
  • A board-ready recommendation

The diagnostic informs where to focus and what to investigate next. Validated business cases, detailed design and implementation are scoped as further work.

02

Operating Model and Transformation Design

For organisations that know where the opportunity is but need to redesign how the business will deliver it.

Six to ten weeks, depending on breadth Fixed fee, scoped per engagement

Typical outputs

  • Target customer journeys, products and propositions
  • Target workflows, decision rights and escalation routes
  • Roles, teams, capabilities and accountability
  • Human, AI and automation boundaries
  • Data, technology and governance requirements
  • A performance and value-realisation model
  • A sequenced transformation roadmap and pilot specifications

Product and digital transformation sit inside this work. Where validation is in scope, the tests, success measures and criteria to proceed, adapt or stop are agreed before testing starts.

03

Transformation Leadership

Embedded, fractional or interim leadership to mobilise and deliver a defined priority transformation.

Tied to an agreed outcome, scope and decision rights

This can include

  • Setting up the transformation and its operating rhythm
  • Aligning executives and resolving difficult decisions
  • Establishing teams, ownership and measures
  • Coordinating internal teams and specialist partners
  • Removing delivery barriers
  • Tracking value and adapting the plan
  • Transferring capability into the organisation

04

CEO, Board and Investor Advisory

Ongoing access to independent senior judgement on AI, product, technology and transformation decisions.

Retainer for continuing access

Intentionally small and senior: regular decision sessions, targeted analysis and constructive challenge.

For boards and non-executives, an independent view on opportunity, investment, governance, readiness and execution risk. For private-equity and growth investors, AI and product value-creation assessment, due diligence and portfolio-company mobilisation.

Fit

Who we work with

CEOs and leadership teams of established, knowledge- or service-intensive businesses, where AI could change costs, capacity, speed, products or customer value, and the route from experimentation to transformation is unclear.

The strongest fit

  • Real product, service or operational complexity
  • Enough scope for operating-model change to affect performance
  • Direct access to a CEO and leadership team able to act on decisions
  • A need for senior transformation capability without a permanent executive hire

What a useful engagement needs

  • An executive sponsor
  • Access to the relevant evidence and people
  • Budget for the agreed work
  • Willingness to change how the business operates

We confirm all of this during scoping, before a fee is agreed.

Who does what

We lead the agreed diagnosis, design, executive alignment and transformation leadership. Your teams bring business knowledge and deliver the changes assigned to them. Specialist partners contribute where needed, with responsibilities agreed before delivery begins.

Common questions

Before you get in touch

  1. 01Are you an AI consultancy? We're an AI and business-transformation advisory. AI is the catalyst; the work is on economics, products, customers, organisation and execution.
  2. 02Do you implement things? Yes. We lead agreed transformation work hands-on, while your teams and specialist partners deliver the changes assigned to them. Scope, decision rights, resources and accountability are agreed before work begins.
  3. 03How are you different from a large consultancy? You work directly with a senior operator who can move from board-level choices into product, operating-model and delivery detail. Small, accountable and practical, with specialists added only where the problem needs them.
  4. 04Why not start with AI training or an automation build? Those can be right when the problem and the expected value are clear. The diagnostic is for when leadership first needs to decide where to focus and what should change.
