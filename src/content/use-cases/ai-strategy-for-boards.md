## Strategy is the short list

Most documents called an AI strategy are inventories. They describe what is possible and what is under way, and they leave the choosing to later.

A board needs the opposite. It needs the few changes that would move the business, the organisational change each depends on, the risks, and a plan for the first ninety days with people's names on it. It also needs to see how firm each number is, because a hypothesis and a validated case deserve different amounts of money.
