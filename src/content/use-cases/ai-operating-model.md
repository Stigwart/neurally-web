## Start with who does the work

The centre of an AI operating model is a decision most organisations never make explicitly: for each piece of work, who or what does it.

Some work stays with a person because it needs judgement or carries accountability. Some is done by a person with AI alongside. Some is handed to AI inside agreed limits, with an owner who can review and reverse it. And some should stop.

Make that allocation deliberately, workflow by workflow, and the roles, skills, systems and measures that need to change become clear. Leave it to chance and each team answers differently.
