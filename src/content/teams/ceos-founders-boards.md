## What a board means by an AI strategy

When a board asks for an AI strategy it is rarely asking about AI. It wants to know whether the business is about to be outrun, whether money is being wasted, and whether anyone is in control.

Those are investment and governance questions. A useful answer names a small number of changes, says what each is worth and how sure anyone can be, and puts a person's name beside each. Every Neurally engagement starts from a version of the same question: what could this business do now that it couldn't two years ago?
