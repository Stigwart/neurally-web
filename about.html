Career evidence

What the experience looks like

These are outcomes from roles Stuart held before founding Neurally. They are not Neurally client case studies. Broader commercial results reflect whole teams and shouldn't be read as the effect of AI alone.

Regulated consumer platform

AI strategy and operating-model redesign at Compare the Market

Situation
A major regulated consumer platform needed to turn AI ambition into a coherent customer, product and organisational strategy.
Role
Authored the consumer AI and app strategy, secured board support and created a three-horizon roadmap. Built the Product AI and Product Operations functions and redesigned how the product organisation worked.
AI outcomes
Launched the company's first consumer-facing AI assistant and first ChatGPT app for insurance, within an FCA-regulated environment. A 50% reduction in discovery-to-development time was reported for the AI-enabled product-development model.
Wider app outcomes
App revenue share rose from 9.3% to 11.0%, monthly active users reached approximately 918,000, and the iOS category ranking rose from 39th to 9th.
B2B2C platform

New revenue and data products at Fresha

Situation
A global platform serving more than 100,000 businesses needed to grow and diversify its embedded-finance and data-product portfolio.
Role
Led strategy, execution and go-to-market across payments, banking, insights and payroll, with P&L accountability and a 46-person multi-location organisation.
AI and data outcomes
Built an ML- and AI-enabled subscription data product that reached more than 2,000 businesses and $75,000 in monthly revenue within six months.
Wider commercial outcomes
Revenue grew by $20 million, or 62%. A new banking platform lifted partner-onboarding conversion from 50% to 80%; a payroll product reached 1,900 businesses, more than $52,000 monthly revenue and 97% retention.
Consumer marketplaces

Product-led growth at ClearScore, Not On The High Street and Gumtree

Situation
Consumer marketplaces needed new propositions, stronger retention and more effective digital growth.
Role
Led product strategy and multi-disciplinary organisations.
Outcomes
At ClearScore, an Open Banking-powered product added 500,000 users with 60% monthly retention, and a merchandisable app experience lifted marketplace conversion by 28%. At Not On The High Street, product changes contributed to 45% new-user growth, an 8% conversion uplift and 130% app MAU growth. At Gumtree, a new Jobs vertical grew to one-third of UK revenue.
