# Claims ledger

Every factual claim on the site, and where it comes from. If a claim isn't in
this table it shouldn't be on the site. When you change a number or a fact,
change it here in the same commit.

**Sources**

- **CP §n** — `docs/fable/context-pack.md`, section n. The context pack is a
  condensed copy of the Drive doc `neurally-overview-v2.1.md` and the previous
  site, prepared by Stuart for this rebuild.
- **Old site** — the static site on `main` before this rebuild (file named).
- **External** — a public primary source, linked.

Where figures live in code: the engagement ladder is `src/data/engagements.ts`,
career history and figures are `src/data/career.ts`, FAQs are
`src/data/faqs.ts`, and landing-page claims are in the front matter of
`src/content/**`.
