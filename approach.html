Approach

We start from the business, not the technology

AI can do more than make existing tasks faster. It can create new products and revenue models, remove whole processes, and give smaller teams the reach of much larger ones. Those gains rarely come from technology alone. They come when customer value, products, processes, people, data, measures and organisation design change together.

Three questions

What we help leadership teams answer

  1. 01 Where is the advantage? Find where AI, technology and changing customer behaviour could create strategic or economic value. Test each opportunity against evidence, feasibility, risk and your organisation's ability to act.
  2. 02 What must change? Translate the strongest opportunities into changes to products, customer journeys and how the business operates, including roles, decisions, data, technology and governance.
  3. 03 How will we realise it? Move from recommendation to execution: prove the riskiest assumptions, mobilise the right work, set ownership and measures, and lead the transformation where needed.

The path

Decide, design, prove, transform, transfer

You can stop after any stage. Each one produces useful outputs that belong to you.

  1. 01

    Decide

    Choose the few opportunities worth pursuing and set out an initial value hypothesis for each.

  2. 02

    Design

    Redesign the business system around those opportunities.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Test the riskiest assumptions through bounded work with agreed success measures, then decide whether to proceed, adapt or stop.

  4. 04

    Transform

    Mobilise and deliver the changes that create value.

  5. 05

    Transfer

    Leave your team with the capability and operating rhythm to continue without dependency.

Investment decisions

How we size an opportunity

For each priority we set out the expected outcome, current performance where the evidence exists, and the assumptions behind the potential improvement. Implementation and running costs, feasibility, readiness, dependencies, adoption, quality and risk all shape the likely value and how long it takes to arrive.

We keep revenue, cost savings, released capacity and customer outcomes separate. Time saved creates capacity. It only becomes a cash saving when spending is reduced or avoided.

Evidence gaps and confidence are recorded against every estimate, so leadership can tell an initial value hypothesis from a validated investment case.

Governance

Built for regulated, board-governed businesses

AI decisions inside regulated or board-governed businesses carry accountability that a generic AI rollout doesn't. Every engagement is designed to hold up under that scrutiny from the outset, not have it bolted on afterwards.

Human and AI accountability

Every workflow redesign sets out who is accountable when AI is involved: what stays with a person, what's automated, and how a decision can be reviewed and reversed.

FCA Consumer Duty and conduct risk

For financial-services and insurance clients, opportunities and designs are tested against Consumer Duty outcomes and conduct-risk implications before they reach a board.

UK GDPR and data governance

Data use, retention and third-party processing are considered as part of scoping, not left to a separate compliance pass afterwards.

EU AI Act exposure

Where a product or workflow could fall under EU AI Act risk categories, that's flagged early enough to shape design decisions, not just disclosed at the end.

Board-level oversight

Recommendations are structured for board and investor scrutiny: assumptions, risk and dependencies are made as visible as the opportunity itself.

This isn't theoretical: it's grounded in launching the first consumer AI assistant and the first ChatGPT app for insurance inside an FCA-regulated, Consumer Duty environment, before founding Neurally — see the career evidence.

Principles

What every engagement holds to

Business value before technology

Every engagement starts with an outcome, an economic hypothesis or a strategic decision. Technology choices follow from that.

Redesign before automation

Automating a poor process preserves the wrong work. We ask first what should be removed, simplified or redesigned.

Deliberate allocation of work

For every important workflow: what value is it meant to create, what should stop first, and what should stay human, be augmented, be automated or be delegated within defined controls?

Evidence before scale

The most important assumptions are tested through bounded work with explicit success measures before large investment is committed.

Capability transfer

Outputs, decisions and working methods belong to you. We leave your leaders and teams able to continue the work.

What we don't do

Boundaries

  • We don't do standalone software builds, general AI training or tool resale.
  • We don't provide recruitment or additional staffing. Specialist partners join only where the problem needs them, with their scope agreed up front.
  • Fractional or interim leadership is scoped to a fixed term and outcome. Open-ended functional cover isn't what we offer.
